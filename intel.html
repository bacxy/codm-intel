01 / Fundamentals

What is CODM?

Call of Duty: Mobile is a free-to-play mobile first-person shooter with several combat formats and a deep weapon customization system.

🎯

Core gameplay loop

Choose a mode, select a weapon and operator setup, complete the objective, earn score and improve your performance. Winning is usually about positioning and decisions—not only aim.

🧰

Loadouts

A loadout combines a primary weapon, secondary weapon, operator skill, three perks, lethal and tactical equipment, and a set of scorestreaks in Multiplayer.

📈

Progression

Play matches and events to earn account XP, weapon XP, camos and other rewards. Ranked modes use competitive tiers and seasonal progression.

06 / Improvement

Win more through decisions

Build repeatable habits that work across weapons and seasons.

👁️

Crosshair placement

Keep your aim near the height where an enemy is likely to appear. Pre-aim common corners rather than reacting from the floor.

🧱

Use cover

Expose as little of your character as possible. After firing, reset behind cover instead of standing still in the same angle.

🧭

Map awareness

Use the minimap, teammate positions, sound cues and kill feed. Predict where enemies may rotate from instead of watching only one lane.

🎮

Practise deliberately

Spend a short session tracking moving targets, controlling recoil and practising turns. Pick one weakness to improve per session.

🤝

Play the objective

In Hardpoint and Domination, kills matter most when they create space or protect an objective. Rotate early and coordinate trades.

🔊

Audio and information

Use comfortable audio settings and learn common sound cues. Avoid relying on audio alone; combine it with the minimap and team information.

Multiplayer checklist

  • Know the objective and the next likely fight.
  • Pre-aim corners and avoid sprinting into every angle.
  • Reload behind cover when possible.
  • Use tactical equipment to create an advantage, not randomly.
  • Review deaths: positioning, timing, aim or information?

Battle Royale checklist

  • Choose a landing area based on your squad and risk tolerance.
  • Loot efficiently; do not stay exposed sorting inventory.
  • Rotate before the zone forces a predictable route.
  • Keep a close-range option and a weapon for distance.
  • Use terrain and cover; avoid unnecessary fights outside the zone.
07 / Player language

CODM glossary

Common terms used in guides, ranked lobbies and team communication.

ADSAim Down Sights: aiming through the weapon's sights.
Hip-fireFiring without aiming down sights.
TtkTime to kill: how long it takes to eliminate a target once shots begin landing.
RecoilWeapon movement during firing; attachments and control technique can affect it.
BRBattle Royale, the large-map survival mode.
MPMultiplayer, including objective and team-based modes.
HUDHeads-up display: on-screen buttons and information layout.
GyroGyroscope aiming, which uses device movement to adjust aim.
Spawn trapControlling routes and pressure around an enemy spawn area.
RotationMoving to the next objective or safe position before it becomes urgent.
TradeEliminating an enemy who just eliminated a teammate, reducing the opponent's advantage.
MetaThe weapons, tactics or setups currently considered especially effective.
08 / Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Useful reminders for building a setup and improving consistently.

What is the best gun in CODM?

There is no permanent best weapon. Balance patches, range, map, mode and player skill change what performs well. Choose a weapon you can control and check current in-game performance.

Should I use two, three or four fingers?

Start with the layout you can control. Three or four fingers can make it easier to aim, fire, jump and slide with less conflict between actions, but a new layout needs practice.

Should I turn on gyroscope?

It is optional. Some players use gyro for fine recoil corrections; others prefer touch-only aiming. Test it in Training Mode and keep it only if it improves consistency.

How do I improve in Ranked?

Learn objective timings and map routes, keep your crosshair ready, play near teammates when trading matters, and avoid repeat challenges that give the enemy easy kills.

Are these exact current-season meta builds?

No. This guide provides weapon roles and build priorities. Exact weapon balance changes with updates, so test current stats in-game.
